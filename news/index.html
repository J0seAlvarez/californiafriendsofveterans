Petaluma Veterans Day Parade 2026

The largest Veterans Day Parade north of the Golden Gate returns to historic downtown Petaluma on Tuesday, November 11, 2026, at 1 p.m. — with the theme “Celebrating 250 Years of the United States of America.”

Official parade site → Parade details at Visit Petaluma

This year

2026 parade details

When: Tuesday, November 11, 2026. Pre-parade music in Walnut Park at noon; the parade begins at 1 p.m.; the post-parade program at the Walnut Park gazebo follows at about 2:45 p.m.

Route: Starting at Walnut Park at D Street, up Fourth and Kentucky Streets to Washington Street, and back to Walnut Park via Petaluma Boulevard.

Theme: “Celebrating 250 Years of the United States of America.”

Grand Marshal: Andrew LeMarQuand, Commander of VFW Post 1929.

Highlights: Petaluma Riding and Driving Club Color Guard, U.S. Coast Guard Training Center Petaluma (Two Rock), American Legion Riders and RIP City Riders, Northbay Military Vehicle Club, Model A Club, Ferrari Club, a flyover of vintage aircraft — including the landing of a Vietnam-era Huey helicopter — and food vendors. The post-parade program includes the Pledge of Allegiance, a prayer, “God Bless America,” speakers, and the U.S. Air Force Band of the Golden West.

History

How the parade began

The parade traces back to 1967, when World War I veteran Julius Forcucci and two of his WWI buddies walked the route together in downtown Petaluma. From 1967 to 1983 the three walked together; then one friend died and the other fell ill, so Forcucci walked alone carrying an American flag in 1984 and 1985.

In 1986, 50 veterans joined him — and the annual parade began. Forcucci died on September 7, 1988, at age 94; at that year's parade, a riderless horse with its stirrups tied to the saddle led the procession in his honor.

The parade has grown ever since: 100 entries by the early 1990s, and by 2019 more than 200 entries with over 2,000 participants and an estimated crowd of 40,000. Today it is recognized as one of the best Veterans Day parades in the country and the largest north of the Golden Gate. It has always been held on the same day at the same time: November 11 at 1 p.m.

Recent years

Themes and grand marshals

  • 2026 — Theme: “Celebrating 250 Years of the United States of America.” Grand Marshal: Andrew LeMarQuand, Commander of VFW Post 1929.
  • 2025 — Theme: “A Salute to the Military Women.” Grand Marshal: Redwood Empire Unit 77 of Military Women Across the Nation.
  • 2024 — Theme: “A Salute to the United States Coast Guard,” recognizing the Coast Guard Training Center just west of Petaluma. Grand Marshal: CDR Kathryn Cyr, USCG Executive Officer.
  • 2023 — Theme: “Salute to Iraq & Afghanistan Veterans,” marking the 20th anniversary of the start of the Iraq War. Grand Marshal: Army Colonel Chris Kolenda.
  • 2021 — Theme: “Welcome Home Vietnam Veterans,” honoring those who served from 1961 to 1975, with a special tribute to the 15 Petaluma men who lost their lives in Vietnam.
