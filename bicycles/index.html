Bicycles

By delusnvet / December 19, 2019

Today we dropped off 7 bicycles to the Healdsburg VFW who supporting the Healdsburg town with Christmas food and toys. Just glad we could help.

We also dropped of 2 bicycles to a young veteran and his family We also help them with Christmas grocery’s and some gifts for the family. We are so happy we can do these things with love and caring from fellow veterans
