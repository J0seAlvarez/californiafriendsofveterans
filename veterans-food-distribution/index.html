Veterans Food Distribution

Free food for those
who served

Every Thursday — drive-thru and walk-up food distribution for veterans and active duty military at the VA Clinic in Santa Rosa.

Distribution Details

VA Clinic South — Santa Rosa

📍

Location

2285 Challenger Way
Santa Rosa, CA 95407

📅

Schedule

Every Thursday
1:00 PM – 3:30 PM

🚗

Service Type

Drive-Thru & Walk-Up

🎖️

Who Can Attend

All Veterans & Active Duty Military

What to Know

Before you come

🛍️

Bring Your Own Bags

Please bring your own bags or a cart to carry your groceries home.

🥫

Groceries To Go

USDA food is open to all — a great selection of essential grocery items available every week.

👴

Senior Basket

A box of staple food items available for low-income seniors aged 60 or older.

🪪

Bring Your ID

Please bring a valid ID if you need to apply for services on-site.
