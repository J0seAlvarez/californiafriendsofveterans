Upcoming Events

Liberty School Veterans Program — Tuesday, November 10, 2026

Be there by 10:00 am. Liberty School, Petaluma. Nov. 10 is a very special day for our veterans as Liberty School in Petaluma puts on a Veterans program for all veterans that come to see.

Petaluma Veterans Day Parade & Flyover — Wednesday, November 11, 2026

Historic Downtown Petaluma, California. The largest parade in northern California. Free for spectators.

  • 11:00 am — Parade entries stage, 4th Street (D to I Streets)
  • 12:00–1:00 pm — Walnut Park music, featuring the USAF Commanders Jazz Combo and music in the gazebo
  • 1:00 pm — Parade starts, from Walnut Park and proceeds through downtown Petaluma
  • 2:45 pm — Post-parade program at Walnut Park, with guest speakers, a prayer, and the Pledge of Allegiance

California Friends will be giving FREE sandwiches, pizza, burritos, and water to all veterans at the parade.

National Wreaths Across America Day — Saturday, December 19, 2026

10:00 am. Cypress Hill Cemetery. Remember, Honor, Teach. Help California Friends of Veterans, Inc. honor local heroes. Sponsor a wreath today to be placed on a veteran's grave this December at Cypress Hill Cemetery. To make a donation online, visit wreathsacrossamerica.org/CA0662P.
